# University AI accountability audit

Status of this directory: local research, completed 2026-11-18. Nothing here has been submitted to USAC, UCSA, UCLA, the University of California, a court, or a school district. No officer has been contacted. No experiment was run.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `evidence-brief.md` | One-page brief. Written so another university, a school district, or a standards project can reuse the evidence rule without adopting UCLA procedure. |
| `usac-resolution-draft.md` | Draft resolution in the form USAC uses. **Proposal. Not sponsored. Not on an agenda.** |
| `audit-memorandum.md` | Full record: policies read, protections, gaps, disputes, statistics recommendation, November 24 meeting rules, and the source log. |

Labels used throughout:

- **Verified** means the cited page, opinion, docket entry, or PDF was opened and the statement is taken from that text.
- **Reported** means a journalist, vendor, or secondary docket index said it. It is not treated as a court finding or as an institutional admission unless the quotation is explicitly theirs.
- **Unverified** means the point was not confirmed against a primary document.
- **Proposal** means text this audit suggests. It is not existing policy.
