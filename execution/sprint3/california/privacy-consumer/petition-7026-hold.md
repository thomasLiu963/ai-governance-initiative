Petition to amend a regulation

I am an interested person. I petition the California Privacy Protection Agency to amend one citation in title 11, California Code of Regulations, section 7026, subsection (g).

1. Substance of the amendment requested

Amend section 7026(g) by replacing “section 7025, subsection (b)(6)” with “section 7025, subsection (c)(6).”

No other word in section 7026, and no other section, is requested to be changed.

2. Reason for the request

Section 7026(g), in the regulations effective January 1, 2026, requires a business to provide a way for the consumer to confirm that a request to opt out of sale or sharing has been processed. The example tells the business it may display “Opt-Out Request Honored” “in accordance with section 7025, subsection (b)(6).”

Section 7025(b) states when a signal is a valid request. It has paragraphs (1) and (2). It has no paragraph (6). Section 7025(c)(6) is the requirement to display whether the business processed an opt-out preference signal, and the example in that paragraph is the same phrase, “Opt-Out Request Honored,” tied to section 7026(g).

A reader who follows the citation in section 7026(g) does not find a display rule. A reader who follows section 7025(c)(6) does. The Agency’s publication “7 Things to Know Before 2026 CCPA Updates Take Effect” cites sections 7025(c)(6) and 7026(g) together for this example.

The requested amendment makes section 7026(g) cite the subsection that contains the example. If the Agency concludes that the change has no regulatory effect, it may use the procedure in title 1, California Code of Regulations, section 100. If the Agency concludes that subsection (b)(6) was intended, please deny the petition in writing and identify the words of that paragraph.

3. Authority to take the action

Civil Code section 1798.185 authorizes the Agency to adopt regulations implementing the California Consumer Privacy Act, including regulations for an opt-out preference signal under subdivision (a)(18). Section 7026 was adopted under that authority. Government Code section 11340.6 authorizes an interested person to petition for the amendment.

Please respond as Government Code section 11340.7 requires, including written notice of receipt and, within 30 days, a written denial stating the reasons or scheduling of the matter for public hearing under Article 5 (commencing with section 11346). Please send the written decision to the Office of Administrative Law for publication in the California Regulatory Notice Register as section 11340.7(d) requires.
