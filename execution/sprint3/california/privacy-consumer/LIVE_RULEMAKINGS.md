The proposal amends registration, list selection, access, hashing, deletion, suppression, and status reporting, and it adds the audit article required by Civil Code section 1798.99.86(e). The statement of reasons says the 30-calendar-day access cycle restates Assembly Bill 883 (Stats. 2026, Ch. 507), which changes the statutory interval from 45 days to 30 days on January 1, 2027. It also says the final sentence of section 7610(a)(3)(C), the 45-day lock on changing list selection, is struck.
