Comment on the proposed Early Intervention Services regulations
Notice published November 6, 2026

I am an interested person. I comment on three sentences in the express terms posted by the Department. I do not ask the Department to add a rule about artificial intelligence, machine translation, or any new early intervention service.

1. Section 52161(a) and its note

Please do not replace “a reasonable time before” with “by certified mail at least 30 days prior to.”

Section 52161(a) should read:

Written notice shall be given to the parent of an infant or toddler, eligible or suspected to be eligible to receive early intervention services, a reasonable time before an Early Start Program proposes, or refuses, to initiate or change:

(1) The identification, evaluation, assessment or placement of the infant or toddler; or

(2) Early intervention services to the infant or toddler and the infant’s or toddler’s family.

Please replace the note so that it cites the current parent-notice sections:

NOTE: Authority cited: Sections 95009 and 95028, Government Code. Reference: Section 95007(g), Government Code; Section 1435(a)(13) and Section 1439(a)(6) and (7), Title 20 United States Code; and Section 303.421, Title 34 Code of Federal Regulations.

Reason. Title 34, Code of Federal Regulations, section 303.421(a), requires prior written notice a reasonable time before the proposed or refused action. Title 20, United States Code, section 1439(a)(6) and (7), is the statute that requires that notice and requires that it inform the parents in their native language unless that is clearly not feasible. The current note cites section 303.403(a), section 303.403(b), and section 303.403(c)(1) and (2). Section 303.403 now defines destruction of a record, early intervention records, and a participating agency. Subsection (c) has no paragraphs (1) and (2).

Sections 303.231 and 303.234 require the Secretary of Education to use certified mail with a state lead agency in a federal eligibility proceeding. They do not govern notice to a parent. The Department’s notice for the January 7, 2027 hearing states that section 303.421 does not specify a 30-day period or certified mail.

Proposed section 52104(h) requires written notice of an individualized family service plan meeting no less than seven calendar days from the meeting, “pursuant to the general notice requirements contained in Section 52161,” and it waives those seven days when the parent asks for a meeting on shorter notice. A notice cannot be the section 52161 notice and also be a seven-day notice, or a shorter notice the parent requested, if section 52161 requires certified mail at least thirty days prior. Please keep “a reasonable time before” so those sentences can be followed together.

The same express terms keep section 52086(a): the initial evaluation and assessment must be completed within 45 days of the referral. Title 34, Code of Federal Regulations, section 303.342(a), requires the meeting to develop the initial individualized family service plan within that 45-day period. Section 303.342(d) requires the meeting in a setting and at a time convenient for the family, and written notice early enough before the meeting that the family can attend. Section 52161(a) is the notice given before the program proposes to initiate an evaluation, an assessment, a placement, or a service. Certified mail sent at least 30 days before that proposal uses most of the 45 days. Section 52162(a) still requires the parent’s written consent before the initial evaluation. A reasonable time can be given inside that sequence. A fixed 30-day certified-mail period cannot, unless the program misses the period section 52086 states.

Certified mail may still be used. Please do not make certified mail, or a 30-day wait, the definition of the notice. A parent who cannot sign for or collect certified mail has not received the notice section 52161(c) requires the program to translate and to make sure the parent understands. The parent who asks for a meeting on shorter notice, which proposed section 52104(h)(1) tells the program to try to accommodate, would still be stopped by a 30-day rule in section 52161.

The Department’s notice for the January 7, 2027 hearing states that section 303.421 requires prior written notice a reasonable time before the action and does not specify a 30-day period or certified-mail delivery. The additional state standard should be one the rest of this proposal can be followed with.

Please also change “from the scheduled IFSP meeting” in section 52104(h) to “before the scheduled IFSP meeting.” The seven-day period is advance notice. “From” does not say that.

Please do not change section 52161(c). The requirement that the notice be understandable, in the language of the parent’s choice unless that is clearly not feasible, translated, understood, and documented should stay as the express terms state it.

2. Section 52112(b)(2) and its note

In the sentence that requires notice to the local educational agency of an individualized family service plan meeting requiring attendance of a representative of that agency “pursuant to 34 CFR 300.132,” please replace “300.132” with “300.124.”

Please delete “303.148,” from the note of section 52112. Leave the citation to section 303.209.

Reason. Section 300.132 is the basic requirement for serving parentally placed private-school children with disabilities. It does not require a local educational agency to attend a Part C transition conference. Section 300.124 is “Transition of children from the Part C program to preschool programs.” Subsection (c) says each affected local educational agency will participate in transition planning conferences arranged by the lead agency. Section 303.148 does not appear in the current text of title 34, Code of Federal Regulations, part 303. Section 303.209 is the current Part C transition section, and the express terms already cite it.

If the Department is deleting the sentence that cites section 300.132, please show that deletion in the final text. If the sentence remains, the citation should be section 300.124. I do not ask the Department to change who attends the conference or the transition timing the express terms already propose.

Please respond to this comment in the final statement of reasons as Government Code section 11346.9 requires.
