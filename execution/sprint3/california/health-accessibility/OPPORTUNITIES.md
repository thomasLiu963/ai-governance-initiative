# Opportunities

Date of research: 2026-11-19. Nothing here was mailed or filed.

The screen kept one opportunity. It is a comment on an open rulemaking. It is not a petition to invent an artificial-intelligence rule. The sections already tell a regional center or local educational agency what notice a parent must receive before a change in an infant’s or toddler’s early intervention services. The proposal would make one of those sentences cite the wrong federal rule and would make two timing sentences impossible to follow together.

## O-1. Department of Developmental Services — title 17, sections 52161, 52104, and 52112

**Instrument:** a written comment on the noticed rulemaking, Government Code section 11346.8. Not a petition under sections 11340.6 and 11340.7. The department has already published the notice and set the hearing. Draft: `comment-dds-early-start.md`.

**Proceeding:** Early Intervention Services. Notice File as published in the California Regulatory Notice Register, November 6, 2026, number 45-Z, and cross-referenced on November 13, 2026, number 46-Z, because the first printing was placed under the wrong heading. The department’s notice PDF that matches those dates is https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/NoticeProposedRulemakingAction_2026.pdf. Express terms: https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/ExpressTermsOfTheProposedRegulations.pdf. Initial statement of reasons: https://www.dds.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/InitialStatementOfReasons.pdf. Comments must be received by January 7, 2027. The hearing is January 7, 2027, from 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., by Zoom or in the California Room, 1215 O Street, Suite 110, Sacramento.

**What the current code says.** Title 17, section 52161(a), gives the parent written notice “a reasonable time before” a regional center or local educational agency proposes or refuses to initiate or change identification, evaluation, assessment, placement, or early intervention services. Subsection (c) requires the notice in words the general public can understand and in the language of the parent’s choice, unless that is clearly not feasible, and it requires translation, an understanding check, and written evidence. The note cites title 34, Code of Federal Regulations, sections 303.403(a), 303.403(b), and 303.403(c)(1) and (2).

Those federal subsections are not the notice rule. Section 303.403, as displayed on the eCFR on 2026-11-19, defines destruction of a record, early intervention records, and a participating agency. Subsection (c) has no paragraphs (1) and (2). The prior-notice rule was moved to section 303.421 in the Part C revision effective October 28, 2011. Section 303.421(a) requires prior written notice a reasonable time before the proposed or refused action. Section 303.421(c) requires the notice in the parent’s native language unless that is clearly not feasible. Title 20, United States Code, section 1439(a)(6) and (7), is the statute those federal sections implement. The California note cites “1439(6)-(7)” without “(a).” Section 1439’s notice paragraphs are in subsection (a).

Title 17, section 52112(b)(2), tells the service coordinator to notify the school district of an individualized family service plan meeting that requires a district representative “pursuant to 34 CFR 300.132.” Section 300.132, as displayed on 2026-11-19, is the basic requirement for serving parentally placed private-school children. It does not require a district representative to attend a Part C transition meeting. Section 300.124 is titled “Transition of children from the Part C program to preschool programs,” and subsection (c) says each affected local educational agency will participate in transition planning conferences arranged by the lead agency. The note to section 52112 cites section 303.148. A search of the current eCFR Part 303 text fetched on 2026-11-19 found no section 303.148. The current transition section is 303.209. The 1997-era section 303.148 is an old transition rule that the 2011 revision replaced.

**What the proposal would do.** The express terms replace “a reasonable time before” in section 52161(a) with notice “by certified mail at least 30 days prior to” the Early Start program proposing or refusing the change. The initial statement of reasons says certified mail “aligns with” sections 303.231 and 303.234. Those sections require the United States Secretary of Education to send certified mail to a state lead agency before finding the state ineligible for a Part C grant, and they require certified mail of the hearing decision in that federal proceeding. They are not the parent-notice rule. The department’s own notice of proposed action says the opposite comparison: section 303.421 requires notice a reasonable time before the action and does not specify 30 days or certified mail.

The same express terms amend section 52104(h) so that written notice of an individualized family service plan meeting is given “no less than seven calendar days from the scheduled” meeting, “pursuant to the general notice requirements contained in Section 52161,” and so that a parent may ask for a meeting on shorter notice and waive the seven days. Seven days, a parent-requested waiver, and a thirty-day certified-mail precondition cannot be the same notice. A meeting that changes services is a proposal to change services. Section 52104(h) says the meeting notice is given pursuant to section 52161.

The express terms keep the section 52112(b)(2) citation to section 300.132 and keep section 303.148 in the note, while adding section 303.209 beside it. A reader who follows 300.132 and a reader who follows 300.124 or 303.209 are not looking at the same duty.

**The change requested.** Three edits, all inside the sections this notice already amends.

1. In section 52161(a), keep “a reasonable time before.” Do not insert certified mail or a 30-day minimum. Use “an Early Start Program proposes, or refuses,” so the new defined term fits the sentence. Replace the note’s citations to sections 303.403(a), 303.403(b), and 303.403(c)(1) and (2) with section 303.421, and cite title 20, United States Code, section 1439(a)(6) and (7).
2. In section 52104(h), say the seven days run “before” the meeting rather than “from” the meeting. Leave the parent-requested shorter-notice waiver in place.
3. In the section 52112 sentence that requires district attendance “pursuant to 34 CFR 300.132,” cite section 300.124. Delete section 303.148 from that section’s note. Leave section 303.209.

Do not change section 52161(c). The language-of-choice, translation, understanding, and written-evidence sentences stay. Do not add a rule about machine translation. The comment does not ask the department to regulate artificial intelligence.

**Who is affected.** The parent of an infant or toddler in Early Start, and the service coordinator who has to give the notice before a change in services or before the transition meeting that determines what happens at age three. Certified mail as the only method does not reach a parent who has no stable mailbox, who cannot collect a postal notice, or who needs the notice in the language subsection (c) already requires, in time to use the seven-day meeting or the shorter meeting the parent asked for. A citation to the private-school rule does not tell a district why it must attend the transition conference. The corrected citations name the federal sections that already describe parent notice and district participation.

**Why this is not a petition.** Government Code section 11346.8 is the procedure for a rulemaking the agency has already noticed. Sections 11340.6 and 11340.7 are the procedure when no such notice is open. Filing a petition now, asking the department to amend sections 52161 and 52112, would ask it to open a second hearing on sections this notice already sets for hearing on January 7, 2027. If the department adopts the certified-mail sentence and the 300.132 citation anyway, a later petition is a different filing and is not drafted here.

## Opportunities examined and not drafted

**Medi-Cal treatment authorization, title 22, section 51003, and Welfare and Institutions Code section 14133.9.** The consultant must decide medical necessity when objective criteria do not resolve the request. No sentence authorizing an automated denial was found. A new artificial-intelligence section would duplicate the consultant duty.

**Medi-Cal notices, title 22, section 50179.** The hearing cross-reference to section 50951(a) resolves. The exception for Welfare and Institutions Code sections 14005.31 and 14005.32 matches a statute that authorizes all-county letters until regulations are adopted.

**Provider enrollment ink signature, title 22, section 51000.30(a)(2)(C).** The bill that would have required electronic signatures was not enacted. A separate statute already allows specified enrollment instructions by bulletin.

**Knox-Keene utilization review, Health and Safety Code section 1367.01(k), and title 28, section 1300.70.** The physician-determination rule is in the statute. The quality-assurance regulation does not contradict it.

**Generative-artificial-intelligence clinical communications, Health and Safety Code section 1339.75, and health-profession titles, Business and Professions Code section 4999.9.** Both statutes operate, and both name the board or the licensing scheme that enforces them. No conflicting regulation was found.

**Health and Human Services language-access policy, October 17, 2025.** Human review of machine translation is already required by the policy before a vital document goes out. The policy is not a code section.

**Public Health diacritical marks on vital records.** Health and Safety Code section 102134(c) authorizes the list without further regulatory action. The department denied a petition on that ground on October 20, 2026, and set a reconsideration hearing for January 18, 2027. A second petition is not drafted.

**Hospice permanent regulations, Z2026-1016-01.** Comments close December 14, 2026. The deadline was read. The proposed text was not. No comment is drafted from a text this pass did not read.

**Department of Aging quarterly reviews, the local-government definition, and area-agency revocation.** The comment periods are closed or the package is already at the Office of Administrative Law. No notice sentence on automated communications was identified.

**Department of Insurance utilization-review guidance and the Civil Rights Council employment citation.** Those are outside this directory. They were closed or drafted in the earlier California files. They are not reopened here.
