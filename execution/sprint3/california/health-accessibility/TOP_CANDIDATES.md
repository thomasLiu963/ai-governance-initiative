## 1. Comment when a named person exists — Department of Developmental Services

**Regulation:** title 17, sections 52161, 52104(h), and 52112, as the department proposes to amend them in the Early Intervention Services rulemaking noticed November 6, 2026.

**Correction:** Section 52161(a) keeps “a reasonable time before an Early Start Program proposes, or refuses.” It does not require certified mail or a 30-day wait. The note cites title 34, Code of Federal Regulations, section 303.421, and title 20, United States Code, section 1439(a)(6) and (7), instead of section 303.403. Section 52104(h) says the seven-day meeting notice runs before the meeting. Section 52112 cites section 300.124 for district participation in the transition conference, instead of section 300.132, and drops section 303.148 from the note.

**Why this ranks first.** It is the only sentence set in this screen that an agency can change in a proceeding already underway, and that a parent and a service coordinator cannot follow together. Prior written notice is how a parent learns that a program proposes or refuses to change an infant’s or toddler’s services, and how the parent gets the procedural safeguards. The proposal would give that notice by certified mail at least thirty days prior, while the same proposal requires the meeting notice that section 52104(h) ties to section 52161 to be seven days, waivable when the parent asks to meet sooner. The statement of reasons cites the Secretary of Education’s certified mail to a state, sections 303.231 and 303.234, as the federal alignment. The parent-notice rule is section 303.421, which the department’s notice correctly says does not require certified mail. The current note points to section 303.403, which now defines records and has no subsection (c)(1) or (c)(2). Separately, section 52112(b)(2) still cites section 300.132 for a district representative’s attendance at the transition meeting. Section 300.132 now governs private-school services. Section 300.124(c) is the transition-conference duty.

The fix does not add a service, a fee, or an artificial-intelligence system. It keeps the notice timing the current regulation and section 303.421 already use, and it makes the citations name the federal sections that govern parent notice and the transition conference.

**Themes:** human agency, for a parent who must be able to receive and understand the notice in time to respond; transparency, for a citation that matches the rule it invokes; human oversight, for the parent-requested meeting the proposal itself allows.

**Burden:** one comment. Three sections already in the notice. Re-read the express terms on the day the comment is sent, because the department may post a modified text.

**Checkpoint.** November 30: the draft can exist. A department response cannot, because the comment period closes January 7, 2027. February 15, 2027: if the comment was received by January 7, it can be in the rulemaking record and the hearing can have occurred. An amended title 17, and the final statement of reasons, are not claimed by that date. The notice year runs to November 6, 2027.

**If the department makes the correction,** the truthful description is: the department amended title 17 so that notice to a parent before a proposed or refused change in early intervention services is still notice given a reasonable time before the change, rather than certified mail sent at least thirty days prior, and so that the transition regulation cites the federal transition-conference section rather than the private-school section.

**If the department rejects the comment,** the truthful description is: the final statement of reasons stated whether section 52161 requires certified mail thirty days before the notice, and whether section 52112(b)(2) requires district attendance because of section 300.132.

The missing name blocks sending. It does not make the comment the wrong instrument.
