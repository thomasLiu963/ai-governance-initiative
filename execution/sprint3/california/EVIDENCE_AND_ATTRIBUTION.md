# Evidence and attribution — California Sprint 3E

Date: 2026-11-19. This file separates what was read, what was drafted, and what an agency has done. An agency has done nothing on these two petitions. They have not been sent.

## Who did the work

The reading, comparison, and drafts in `execution/sprint3/california/` were produced in this project on 2026-11-19 by the Sprint 3E review, using the Sprint 2 California memos as the starting record and re-checking the top candidates against primary sources.

That is not a record that Thomas personally read each page, personally typed each sentence without assistance, or personally tested a system. Signing a letter is the act that adopts it. Until that signature and the mailing exist, a truthful description is: petitions were drafted and held for approval.

Do not write any of the following unless the event has happened and the evidence is linked here:

- that a petition was filed
- that an agency agreed, partnered, sponsored, or endorsed the request
- that the petitioner is employed by the agency or the State of California
- that a regulation was amended
- that a hearing was scheduled
- that a Notice Register decision was published

The public attribution, once a decision exists, is the petitioner’s name in the writing section 11340.7(d) requires. That naming is identification of the person who petitioned. It is not a statement that the agency adopted the person’s view.
