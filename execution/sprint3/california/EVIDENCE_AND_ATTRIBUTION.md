## Public attribution

A rulemaking file is public. A Notice Register decision on a petition must name the petitioner. That naming identifies the person who wrote. It is not employment by the agency, a partnership, a sponsorship, or the agency’s endorsement of the request.

Do not write any of the following unless the event has happened and the writing is saved:

- that a comment or petition was filed
- that an agency agreed with it
- that a hearing was held, unless the hearing date has passed and the record shows it occurred
- that a final statement of reasons adopted the request, unless the statement says that
- that a regulation was amended, unless the code contains the new words
