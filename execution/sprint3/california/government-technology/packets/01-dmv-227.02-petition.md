Petition to amend a regulation
Government Code sections 11340.6 and 11340.7

I am an interested person. I petition the Department of Motor Vehicles to amend three definitions in title 13, California Code of Regulations, section 227.02.

1. Substance of the amendment requested

Amend section 227.02 as follows. Leave every form revision date as it appears in the code on the date of this petition.

(a) In subsection (v), replace “to renew the Driverless Testing Permit” with “to renew the Drivered Testing Permit.”

The purpose clause would then read: to renew the Drivered Testing Permit pursuant to section 227.20(a).

(b) In subsection (w), replace “means a Driverless Testing Permit, form OL 318” with “means a Driverless Testing Permit Application, form OL 318.”

(c) In subsection (x), replace “means a Driverless Testing Permit, form OL 318” with “means a Driverless Testing Permit Application, form OL 318.”

No other section is requested to be changed.

2. Reason for the request

Section 227.02(r) defines a “Drivered Testing Permit” as form OL 315. That permit requires an autonomous-vehicle test driver. Section 227.02(t) defines a “Driverless Testing Permit” as form OL 315A. That permit does not require an autonomous-vehicle test driver in the driver’s seat. Section 227.02(u) defines a “Driverless Testing Permit Application” as form OL 318.

Section 227.02(v) defines a “Drivered Testing Permit Renewal Application” as form OL 311, submitted by a manufacturer that possesses a valid drivered permit, “to renew the Driverless Testing Permit pursuant to section 227.20 (a).” The defined term is the drivered renewal. The clause states the driverless permit. Section 227.02(x) states that the driverless renewal application renews the driverless permit. Only subsection (v) points at the other permit.

Section 227.02(w) defines the “Driverless Testing Permit Operational Parameters Modification Application” as “a Driverless Testing Permit, form OL 318.” Section 227.02(x) defines the “Driverless Testing Permit Renewal Application” as “a Driverless Testing Permit, form OL 318.” Section 227.02(u) defines form OL 318 as the application. Section 227.02(t) defines the driverless permit as form OL 315A. Section 227.42(o) requires form OL 318, as an application, for the changes in paragraphs (o)(1) through (7), and it identifies the permit as form OL 315A.

Section 227.42 states that a valid drivered permit is a condition for maintenance of the driverless permit. A reader who follows subsection (v) uses form OL 311 to renew the authority to test with no person in the driver’s seat. A reader who follows subsections (t) and (u), section 227.20, and section 227.42 uses form OL 318 to renew that authority and uses form OL 311 to renew the permit that requires a test driver. The two readings are in the same article. The manufacturer and the department are the persons who have to apply the definitions. Government Code section 11349(c) requires the regulation to be written so that those persons can understand it.

The requested words do not change the number of miles that must be tested, the duties of a remote driver, or the rules for a heavy vehicle. They make subsection (v) name the permit that the defined term and the drivered form already name, and they make subsections (w) and (x) name an application, which is what subsection (u) and section 227.42(o) already call form OL 318.

If the department intended form OL 311 to renew the driverless permit, this petition asks for a written denial that says so. That intent would have to be reconciled with the name of subsection (v), with subsection (x), and with the condition in section 227.42.

3. Authority

Vehicle Code sections 1651 and 38750 are the authority cited on section 227.02. Section 227.00(a) states that Article 3.7 implements Division 16.6 of the Vehicle Code, commencing with section 38750. Government Code section 11340.6 authorizes an interested person to petition for the amendment. Government Code section 11340.7 requires a written decision.

The definitions are not removed from the Administrative Procedure Act by Government Code section 11340.9. They tell every manufacturer which filing renews which authority to operate on a public road. This petition does not ask the department to change a form. It asks the department to change the regulation that states what the form renews.

Title 1, California Code of Regulations, section 100, permits a change without the Article 5 procedure only when the change does not materially alter a requirement, right, responsibility, condition, prescription, or other regulatory element. The department and the Office of Administrative Law can decide whether this amendment is that kind of change. This petition asks for the amendment either way.

4. Decision requested
