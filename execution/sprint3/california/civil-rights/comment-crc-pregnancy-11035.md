I submit this comment on the Civil Rights Council’s proposed amendments to title 2, California Code of Regulations, division 4.1, chapter 5, subchapter 2, articles 6 and 11, Notice File Number Z2026-1006-03, published in the California Regulatory Notice Register on October 16, 2026.

## The change requested

I ask the Council to correct two designations in the Initial Statement of Reasons. I do not ask the Council to change the express terms on either designation.

1. Under the heading “§11035(p). Qualified employee,” designate the definition that follows as subsection (p) of section 11035. Delete the subsection letter “(s)” from the start of that definition. Do not move “qualified employee” to subsection (s).

The corrected definition block should read:

> § 11035(p). “Qualified employee.” The Council proposes to add a definition for the term “qualified employee,” defined as follows:
>
> (p) “Qualified employee” means either:
>
> (1) an employee who, with or without reasonable accommodation, can perform the essential functions of their position, or if the accommodation is leave, who is reasonably expected to be able to perform the essential functions, with or without an accommodation, at the end of the leave; or
>
> (2) an employee who is unable to perform one or more essential functions if the inability to perform the essential function(s) can be reasonably accommodated through, for instance, the temporary suspension of the essential function(s) and the employee performing the remaining functions of the position, or the remaining functions plus other functions temporarily assigned by an employer; the employee performing the functions of a different job to which they are temporarily transferred; the employee participating in the employer’s light or modified duty program; or other arrangements.

2. In the explanation of current section 11035(a), replace “new subsection 11050(m)(1)” with “new subsection 11035(m)(1).”

No other section is requested to be changed. In particular, do not change the express terms’ designation of “self-confirmation” as section 11035(s), and do not change the citations to section 11035(p) in proposed sections 11040(c)(3) and 11041(g).

## The reason

The express terms designate “qualified employee” as section 11035(p). They designate “self-confirmation” as section 11035(s). Proposed section 11040(c)(3) says that an employee who rejects a reasonable accommodation or transfer necessary to perform an essential function, and who then cannot perform that function, will not be considered “qualified,” as defined in section 11035(p). Proposed section 11040(c)(3)(A) uses the same paragraph. Proposed section 11041(g) does the same for a rejected transfer.

The Initial Statement of Reasons, at the heading “§11035(p). Qualified employee,” says the Council proposes to add that definition, and the definition it then prints begins “(s) ‘Qualified employee.’” Later in the same statement, “§ 11035(s). Self-confirmation” introduces a different definition. The statement of reasons also cites section 11035(p) when it explains proposed sections 11040(c)(3) and 11041(g).

Subsection (p) and subsection (s) cannot both be the qualified-employee definition. Subsection (s) cannot be both that definition and the self-confirmation definition. A reader of the express terms and a reader of the definition block in the statement of reasons are sent to different subsections. The subsection decides whether an employee who cannot perform an essential function remains a person the accommodation and transfer rules protect.

The same statement says that current section 11035(a), “affected by pregnancy,” is moved to new subsection 11035(m)(1), and it then tells the reader to see the explanation of new subsection 11050(m)(1). The explanation is under subsection 11035(m)(1). Section 11050 is the medical-certification section. It does not contain a subsection (m).

I ask the Council, if it believes the express terms should be changed so that “qualified employee” is subsection (s), to deny that course in the final statement of reasons and to state which subsection an employer must use. I ask that the express terms not be changed to (s). That change would place the qualified-employee definition on the letter the express terms already use for self-confirmation, while sections 11040(c)(3) and 11041(g) would still cite section 11035(p).

## Authority

Government Code section 12935(a) authorizes the Council to adopt, promulgate, amend, and rescind regulations that interpret, implement, and apply the Fair Employment and Housing Act. The notice cites sections 12945 and 12945.2 as the statutes this proposal implements and makes specific. Section 12945 requires an employer to provide a reasonable accommodation for an employee for a condition related to pregnancy, childbirth, or a related medical condition, when the employee requests it with the advice of the employee’s health care provider. The qualified-employee definition is part of how the proposal specifies that duty when the employee cannot perform an essential function.

This comment is submitted under the notice and comment procedure for this rulemaking. It is not a petition to open a separate rulemaking.
