# Agency screen — civil rights, employment, education, housing

Date of research: 2026-11-19. Research memo, not a legal opinion. Nothing in this directory was mailed, filed, emailed, or published.

This pass covers statewide agencies that regulate employment discrimination, automated hiring and assessment, civil rights enforcement, education, student privacy and assessment, housing discrimination, professional licensing, and administrative decisions with a comparable consequence. It starts from the California Sprint 2 screen and does not reopen a lead that screen closed unless a later sentence, notice, or statute supplied a new defect.

The optimization test is a truthful, independently checkable institutional act before February 15, 2027, with November 30, 2026 as an early checkpoint. A comment receipt, a form letter, or a denial is not that act. An amended code section, a correction the agency publishes in a final statement of reasons or a modified text, or a corrected operative guidance document can be, if the writing shows the change.

## How a mechanism was assigned

Government Code sections 11340.6 and 11340.7 let an interested person petition a state agency to adopt, amend, or repeal a regulation, and they require a written denial or a noticed hearing within 30 days, unless a statute restricts who may petition or prescribes a different petition. The chapter does not apply to the bodies and documents listed in section 11340.9. A form and its instructions are outside the chapter under section 11340.9(c), but that exemption does not excuse a regulation that is needed to implement the law the form carries out. A document that relates only to an agency’s internal management is outside the chapter under section 11340.9(d).

An open notice of proposed action is the path that can still change text. Government Code section 11346.8 lets the agency change the text if the change is sufficiently related to the noticed proposal, after further notice when section 11346.8 requires it. Section 11346.9 requires a final statement of reasons that responds to the comments. A petition under section 11340.6 is the wrong instrument for text that is already in that comment period.

Title 1, California Code of Regulations, section 100 is a change without regulatory effect. It is available only when the Office of Administrative Law agrees the change does not alter a requirement. It is not a way to rewrite a statement of reasons during an open Article 5 proceeding.

Government Code section 11340.5 is the underground-regulation rule. It is a candidate only when an agency is enforcing a standard of general application that meets the definition of a regulation and has not been adopted under the chapter. A guidance document that says it is not mandatory, and that cites the statute making it nonmandatory, is not that case.

## What was not read

This pass did not read every section of titles 2, 5, 8, 16, or 22. A later pass can reopen a closed row by quoting a specific subsection that cannot be applied together with the statute or with another subsection. This pass does not invent that subsection.
