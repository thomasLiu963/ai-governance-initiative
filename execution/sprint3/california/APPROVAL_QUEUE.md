# Approval queue — California Sprint 3E

Date: 2026-11-19. Nothing below is authorized to leave this workspace until the approval line for that item is explicit.

An approval is permission to mail one named letter after the day-of-mailing re-read. It is not permission to email, to post, to combine the letters, or to describe either letter as already filed.

## How to approve

Reply with one of:

- `approve CA-DMV-227.02`
- `approve CA-CRC-11008.1`
- `approve both California petitions`

Anything else is not approval. Approving both authorizes two separate mailings. It does not authorize one combined letter.
