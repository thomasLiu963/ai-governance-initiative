## Before signing

The letters are in the first person. Signing means you have read the quoted regulatory sentences and can explain the request. Drafting assistance does not supply that. Put your name on the letter. Put a mailing address or email on it only if that contact may be public. Leave telephone numbers blank. Do not add medical information, a child’s name, or an account number.
