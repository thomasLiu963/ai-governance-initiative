Petition to amend a regulation
Government Code sections 11340.6 and 11340.7

I am an interested person. I petition the Civil Rights Council to amend one citation in title 2, California Code of Regulations, section 11008.1, subsection (a).

1. Substance of the amendment requested

Amend section 11008.1(a) by replacing “section 11008(i)” with “section 11008(h).”

The subsection would read:

“Automated-Decision System.” A computational process that makes a decision or facilitates human decision making regarding an employment benefit, as defined in section 11008(h) of these regulations. An Automated-Decision System may be derived from and/or use artificial intelligence, machine-learning, algorithms, statistics, and/or other data processing techniques.

No other word in section 11008.1, and no other section, is requested to be changed.

2. Reason for the request

Section 11008.1(a) says an automated-decision system is a computational process that makes a decision or facilitates human decision making “regarding an employment benefit, as defined in section 11008(i).”

Section 11008(h) defines “Employment Benefit.” Section 11008(i) defines “Employment Practice.” “Employment Practice” is any act, omission, policy, or decision of an employer or other covered entity affecting any of an individual’s employment benefits or consideration for an employment benefit. That is a different term.

The citation points at the wrong subsection because a new subsection (a), “Agent,” was added to section 11008 in the automated-decision system rulemaking filed with the Secretary of State on June 27, 2025, and effective October 1, 2025. “Employment Benefit,” which had been subsection (g), became subsection (h). “Employment Practice,” which had been subsection (h), became subsection (i). Section 11008.1(a) kept the letter (i).

The words of section 11008.1(a) name an employment benefit. The citation names the subsection that now defines an employment practice. Both directions cannot be followed. Duties that use the term “automated-decision system,” including sections 11009(f), 11013, 11016, and 11017(e), depend on this definition.

The requested amendment makes the citation name the subsection that defines the term the sentence already uses. It does not add or remove a duty. If the Council concludes that the words “employment benefit” already control, this is a cross-reference correction. If the Council concludes that the citation was intended to incorporate “employment practice,” please deny the petition in writing and say so, because that reading conflicts with the words “employment benefit.”

3. Authority to take the action

Government Code section 12935(a) authorizes the Council to adopt, promulgate, amend, and rescind regulations that interpret, implement, and apply the Fair Employment and Housing Act. Section 11008.1 was adopted under that authority. Government Code section 11340.6 authorizes an interested person to petition for the amendment.

Please respond as Government Code section 11340.7 requires, including written notice of receipt and, within 30 days, a written denial stating the reasons or scheduling of the matter for public hearing under Article 5 (commencing with section 11346). Please send the written decision to the Office of Administrative Law for publication in the California Regulatory Notice Register as section 11340.7(d) requires.
