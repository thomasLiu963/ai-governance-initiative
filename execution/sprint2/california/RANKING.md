# Ranking — California statewide screen

Date: 2026-11-19. Nothing here was mailed or filed. November 30, 2026 is the early checkpoint. February 15, 2027 is the later checkpoint. A written decision under Government Code section 11340.7(a) can fall inside the later checkpoint if a completed letter is received in December 2026. An amended code section is a later event. This ranking does not claim that either code section will be amended by February 15, 2027.

The screen covered employment, privacy, insurance, health-plan quality assurance, state technology policy, housing and state programs, campaign advertising, autonomous-vehicle testing, workplace statutes signed in 2026, browsers, clinical decision support, education, and passenger-service decisions. Most of those targets have no sentence that is both adopted and broken. That is a finding about the code. It is not a finding that the statewide search was the wrong search. Two petitions are ready to review. The rest were closed for a stated reason in `STATEWIDE_SCREENING.md`.

## 1. File when a named person exists — Department of Motor Vehicles

**Regulation:** title 13, California Code of Regulations, section 227.02, subsections (v), (w), and (x). Order to Adopt, Article 3.7, OAL file 2025-0415-04, effective April 28, 2026.

**Correction:** In subsection (v), the drivered renewal application renews the drivered permit, not the driverless permit. In subsections (w) and (x), form OL 318 is the application, not the driverless permit. No other section is requested to change. Form revision dates stay as they appear in the code on the day of mailing.

**Why this ranks first.** A driverless testing permit is the authority to operate on a public road with no person in the driver’s seat. Section 227.42 makes a current drivered permit a condition of keeping that authority. Subsection (v) tells a manufacturer that the drivered renewal form renews the driverless permit. Subsections (w) and (x) call the application form the permit. A manufacturer, a remote driver, a passenger, and the department cannot all follow those sentences and section 227.42. The fix does not add a testing mile, a fee, or a new safety case. It makes the existing permit names point at the permits the rest of the article already uses.

**Themes:** human oversight, and the authorization to operate without a person in the seat.

**Burden:** one letter. Three word-level corrections in one section. Re-read the three subsections on Westlaw, or in the official code, the day the letter is mailed, because an August 2026 section 100 filing changed the OL 318 revision date in section 227.42 and was not shown to have changed these three nouns.

**Checkpoint.** November 30: the draft can exist. A decision cannot, because the 30 days run from receipt. February 15, 2027: a written denial, or a written decision to schedule a hearing, can exist if the letter is received in December and the department follows section 11340.7(a). An amended section 227.02 by that date is not claimed.

**If the department makes the correction,** the truthful description is: the Department of Motor Vehicles amended title 13, section 227.02, so that the application renewing a permit which requires a person in the driver’s seat is no longer defined as the application that renews the permit to test with no person in the driver’s seat, and so that the driverless application forms are no longer defined as the permit.

**If the department denies in writing,** the truthful description is narrower and is still an institutional result: the department denied a petition, in a writing transmitted for the California Regulatory Notice Register, and stated whether subsection (v) means that form OL 311 renews a driverless permit.

## 2. Separate small petition — Civil Rights Council

**Regulation:** title 2, section 11008.1(a). Effective October 1, 2025. Change “section 11008(i)” to “section 11008(h).”

This stays behind the DMV letter because the phase 1 memo already built it, and because a reader who follows the words “employment benefit” and a reader who follows the citation reach different sets of employment decisions. They do not reach different answers about whether a vehicle may operate with no person in the seat. Both letters can be mailed. They are not substitutes. Do not merge them into one petition. They go to different agencies.

**If the Council makes the correction,** the truthful description is: the Civil Rights Council amended title 2, section 11008.1(a), so that the definition of an automated-decision system cites the subsection that defines “employment benefit,” which is the term the sentence already uses.

**If the Council denies in writing,** the description is: the Council denied the petition in a writing for the Notice Register and stated whether “employment benefit” in that sentence is the term defined in section 11008(h) or the term defined in section 11008(i).

The missing name and address block mailing. They do not drop the letter.
