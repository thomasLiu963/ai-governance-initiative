Petition to amend a regulation
Government Code sections 11340.6 and 11340.7

I am an interested person. I petition the Department of Motor Vehicles to amend three definitions in title 13, California Code of Regulations, section 227.02.

1. Substance of the amendment requested

Amend section 227.02 as follows. Leave every form revision date as it appears in the code on the date of this petition.

(a) In subsection (v), replace “to renew the Driverless Testing Permit” with “to renew the Drivered Testing Permit.”

The purpose clause would read: to renew the Drivered Testing Permit pursuant to section 227.20(a).

(b) In subsection (w), replace “means a Driverless Testing Permit, form OL 318” with “means a Driverless Testing Permit Application, form OL 318.”

(c) In subsection (x), replace “means a Driverless Testing Permit, form OL 318” with “means a Driverless Testing Permit Application, form OL 318.”

No other section is requested to be changed. Section 227.20 and section 227.42 are not requested to be changed.

2. Reason for the request

Section 227.02(r) defines a “Drivered Testing Permit” as form OL 315. That permit requires an autonomous-vehicle test driver. Section 227.02(t) defines a “Driverless Testing Permit” as form OL 315A. That permit does not require an autonomous-vehicle test driver in the driver’s seat. Section 227.02(u) defines a “Driverless Testing Permit Application” as form OL 318.

Section 227.02(v) defines a “Drivered Testing Permit Renewal Application” as form OL 311, submitted by a manufacturer that possesses a valid drivered permit, “to renew the Driverless Testing Permit pursuant to section 227.20(a).” The defined term is the drivered renewal. The clause states the driverless permit. Section 227.02(x) states that the driverless renewal application renews the driverless permit. Only subsection (v) points at the other permit.

Section 227.42 states that a valid drivered permit, form OL 315, is a condition for maintenance of a driverless permit, form OL 315A. A reader who follows subsection (v) uses form OL 311 to renew the authority to test with no person in the driver’s seat. A reader who follows subsections (t) and (u), the form names in section 227.20(b), and section 227.42 uses form OL 318 to renew that authority and uses form OL 311 to renew the permit that requires a test driver. Both readings are in the adopted article.

Section 227.02(w) defines the “Driverless Testing Permit Operational Parameters Modification Application” as “a Driverless Testing Permit, form OL 318.” Section 227.02(x) defines the “Driverless Testing Permit Renewal Application” as “a Driverless Testing Permit, form OL 318.” Section 227.02(u) defines form OL 318 as the application. Section 227.02(t) defines the driverless permit as form OL 315A. Section 227.42(o) requires form OL 318, as an application, for the changes in paragraphs (o)(1) through (7), and it identifies the permit as form OL 315A.

These three definitions were adopted in the department’s Order to Adopt for Article 3.7, OAL file 2025-0415-04, effective April 28, 2026. A later change without regulatory effect updated the revision date of form OL 318 in section 227.42. This petition does not ask to freeze or to change any revision date.

The requested amendments make subsection (v) name the drivered permit, and they make subsections (w) and (x) name an application, which is what form OL 318 is in subsection (u) and in section 227.42. They do not add or remove a mile, a fee, a remote-driver duty, or a class of vehicle. If the department concludes that form OL 311 is intended to renew the driverless permit, or that form OL 318 is the driverless permit, please deny the petition in writing and say so. That reading conflicts with the names of the defined terms, with subsection (t), with subsection (u), and with section 227.42.

3. Authority to take the action

Vehicle Code sections 1651 and 38750 are the authority cited on section 227.02. Section 227.00(a) states that Article 3.7 implements Division 16.6 of the Vehicle Code, commencing with section 38750. Government Code section 11340.6 authorizes an interested person to petition for the amendment.

Please respond as Government Code section 11340.7 requires, including written notice of receipt and, within 30 days, a written denial stating the reasons or scheduling of the matter for public hearing under Article 5 (commencing with section 11346). Please send the written decision to the Office of Administrative Law for publication in the California Regulatory Notice Register as section 11340.7(d) requires.
