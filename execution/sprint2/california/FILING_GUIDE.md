# Filing guide

Date: 2026-11-19. This guide does not authorize mailing. Both drafts are marked DRAFT — NOT FILED. Mail one only after a named person has approved that specific letter, signed it, and put a mailing address on it. Do not email either agency unless that agency has expressly said it accepts a Government Code section 11340.6 petition by electronic communication. Government Code section 11340.85(b)(5) requires that express indication. A published address for comments on a noticed proposal is not that indication.

## What the letter must contain

Government Code section 11340.6 requires three things, clearly and concisely: the substance of the amendment, the reason, and the authority for the agency to take the action. Both drafts contain those three parts. Section 11340.7(d) requires the agency’s published decision to identify the party submitting the petition. A letter with a blank name is not a completed petition. A telephone number or email address is not required. Add one only if the person wants it in a public Notice Register decision.

Sign in ink. Date the letter. Keep a copy. Use a method that shows the date of mailing. The 30 days run from receipt, not from mailing.

## What happens after receipt

Within 30 days the agency notifies the petitioner of receipt and either denies the petition in writing, with reasons, or schedules the matter for a public hearing under Article 5. It may grant or deny in part, or take other action it finds warranted, and it must say so in writing. A request for reconsideration, in the form section 11340.6 requires, is due no later than 60 days after the decision. The written decision is transmitted to the Office of Administrative Law for the California Regulatory Notice Register.

Scheduling a hearing is not the amendment. Article 5 notice is at least 45 days. The notice lives for one year. The Office of Administrative Law still approves the text before it is filed with the Secretary of State.

If the agency treats the change as having no regulatory effect, title 1, section 100, gives the Office 30 working days after it receives the submission. The code changes only if the Office approves and files it. Neither draft asks the agency to promise that path.

## How to describe the outcome

Use the sentence that matches the event that occurred.

- Notice Register publication of a denial: the agency denied the petition and stated its reasons. The code is unchanged.
- Notice Register publication of a decision to schedule a hearing: the agency opened Article 5. The code is unchanged until adoption, Office of Administrative Law approval, and filing.
- Amended section 227.02 in the code: the Department of Motor Vehicles amended the section so that the application renewing a permit which requires a person in the driver’s seat is no longer defined as the application that renews the permit to test with no person in the driver’s seat, and so that the driverless application forms are no longer defined as the permit.
- Amended section 11008.1(a) in the code: the Civil Rights Council amended the section so that the definition of an automated-decision system cites the subsection that defines “employment benefit.”

Silence after 30 days is not a grant. A workshop, a staff email, or a docket receipt is not the result section 11340.7 describes.
