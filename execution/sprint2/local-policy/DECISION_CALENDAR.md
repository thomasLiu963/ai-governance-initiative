## How to read a public comment against a vote

USAC: an officer's email by the noon deadline, with the text in the packet, is what gets an action item on the agenda. Speaking in public comment does not.

Pasadena and Tulare: a letter is public comment or packet correspondence. The board votes the policy that is on the agenda. The letter becomes part of the decision only if a member moves the sentences and the board adopts them.

LAUSD committee: registration and two minutes are a comment on the posted item. The full board's later vote, if any, was not scheduled inside this horizon.

Neighborhood councils and the coalitions: a stakeholder request is a request. The presiding officer or the board decides the agenda. A passed motion is that body's position.

UCSA: the AIR form is a request. The chair's acceptance is the agenda. The vote threshold is still unread in the current bylaws.
