# Execution plan

Research date: 2026-11-19. No proposal was submitted. No email was sent. No officer, trustee, or staff member was contacted. This plan does not authorize those steps.

## What February 15 can actually be

The documented act that can exist by February 15, 2027, if sponsors and a board member each agree, is one of these:

- USAC minutes of a resolution passed on January 12 or February 9, 2027. The minutes are the Council's opinion. They are the UCLA record.
- A Tulare County Board minute of December 11, 2026, or of the following meeting if the policy slips, adopting BP 0441 with the added sentences. That policy binds the county office.
- A Pasadena Unified minute of the second reading, on a Thursday that is not yet posted, adopting BP 0441 with the added sentences. That policy binds the district.

A LAUSD committee comment, a UCSA agenda request, and a neighborhood-council request can be on the record by February 15. They are not the adoption.

November 30, 2026 cannot be an adoption date for any of the three. It can be the date Thomas writes down which of the three drafts, if any, may be sent. The Tulare letter is the only draft that has to move in the first days of December if it is going to be inside the December 11 packet. The USAC filing waits until three sponsors exist and until Thursday, January 7, 2027, at noon.

Do not add a false-positive percentage, a detector ban, or a demand for browsing history. Those were considered in the November 18 audit and left out. The model file records the same exclusion.

Do not send the one-page evidence brief or the resolution footnotes until a person has checked each quotation against `audits/university-policy/audit-memorandum.md`. The footnotes were not reopened on November 19.

## Evidence, if a body acts

| --- | --- |
| USAC | The posted minutes and the packet page that lists the three sponsors and the vote. |
| Tulare County | The minutes line "Consideration and Approval, Second Reading" and the adopted policy text. |
| Pasadena Unified | The minutes of the adoption vote and the policy as posted in the district manual. |

A draft, a sent email, and a reply that promises to look at the idea are not results. They can be noted in `execution/SUBMISSION_LOG.md` only after a send is approved and made. This sprint does not write that log entry.

## Hours, in the order they buy a record

The Tulare letter is a few hours once the November 4 text is opened, and it is the only operational vote with a posted meeting inside December. Pasadena is the same letter against a Los Angeles County district, gated on one agenda check. USAC is the UCLA record, and almost all of its hours are the sponsor conversations, not the drafting. The resolution is already written.

December 9 at LAUSD is two hours of comment preparation if the agenda includes a recommendation. It should not be the first hour spent.

UCSA, the neighborhood councils, and the coalitions are later hours. They copy the same clauses after one of the three packets has a decision, or after a current UCSA bylaw and a city council file are found. Spending those hours now produces requests to bodies that have not agendized the subject.
