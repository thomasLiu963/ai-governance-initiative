## After something is actually sent

Record the date, the single method, and the address or confirmation number. Keep the copy that was sent. An agency receipt is evidence of receipt. It is not adoption of the requested text.

By February 15, 2027, the supportable external record for an approved and timely send is that the comment is in the file and that a hearing scheduled before that date has occurred. A final statement of reasons and an amended code section are later events. The Early Start notice runs to November 6, 2027. The Office of Administrative Law notice of September 28, 2026, runs one year under Government Code section 11346.4(b).

A Government Code section 11340.7 writing is available only after a verified petition is mailed and received. This checklist does not approve either petition. Reconsideration of a petition denial would be a new petition, due no later than 60 days after the decision. This checklist does not approve a reconsideration.
