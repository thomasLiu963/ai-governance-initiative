# Approval queue — rapid filings

Date: 2026-11-20. Nothing below leaves this workspace until the approval line for that item is explicit. An approval phrase in `execution/sprint4/california-ai/APPROVAL_QUEUE.md` does not send any letter in this directory. An approval phrase here does not send the insurance comment, the opt-out draft, or a petition.

## How to approve

`approve FCC comment`

Files the public text in `FCC_FINAL.md`, and only that text, after the bracketed name, mailing address, and date are replaced. The filer has accepted that the name and mailing address will be public on ECFS. One submission, CG Docket No. 02-278, on or before November 25, 2026.

`approve OAL comment`

Sends the Office of Administrative Law letter under that heading in `CALIFORNIA_QUICK_WINS.md`. One email or one mailing. Deadline December 9, 2026, 11:59 p.m.

`approve CRC pregnancy comment`

Sends the pregnancy statement-of-reasons letter under that heading in `CALIFORNIA_QUICK_WINS.md`, after the morning-of check in `DEADLINE_CHECKLIST.md` still shows the wrong subsection letter. One email or one mailing. Received by 5:00 p.m. Pacific Time on December 8, 2026.

`approve DDS comment`

Sends the Early Start letter under that heading in `CALIFORNIA_QUICK_WINS.md`, after the express terms still contain the certified-mail sentence and the section 300.132 citation. One mailing or one web-form submission, using the January 7, 2027 notice. Deadline January 7, 2027.

`approve DROP citation`

Sends the data-broker letter under that heading in `CALIFORNIA_QUICK_WINS.md`, after section 7614(b)(2)(B) still cites a subsection that is not the multiple-consumer rule. One email or one mailing. Deadline 5:00 p.m. Pacific Time on January 6, 2027.

`approve the four California comments`

Sends the Office of Administrative Law letter, the pregnancy letter, the Early Start letter, and the DROP letter, each subject to the morning-of check for that letter. It does not file the FCC comment.

`approve FCC and the four California comments`

Sends all five. Each still needs its own name block, its own morning-of check, and its own single method.

Any other reply is not approval. Approval of one item does not approve another.

## Before signing

The letters are in the first person. Signing means the signer has read the quoted regulatory sentences and can explain the request. Put the signer’s name on the letter. Put a mailing address or email on it only if that contact may be public. Leave telephone numbers off. On the Early Start letter, do not add a child’s name, a diagnosis, a regional-center record, or any other medical information.

## Order if several are approved

1. FCC comment. The window closes November 25, 2026. This is the only item that cannot wait until December.
2. Pregnancy comment, if the statement of reasons is still wrong. Received by 5:00 p.m. Pacific Time on December 8, 2026. Skip it if that morning is needed for the Office of Administrative Law letter. The pregnancy letter is a citation correction. The file-and-print letter is the stronger December filing.
3. Office of Administrative Law comment. Written comments close December 9, 2026, at 11:59 p.m.
4. Early Start comment. The record stays open through January 7, 2027. Send it once, after the December letters if those are also approved.
5. DROP citation. The record stays open through January 6, 2027, at 5:00 p.m. Pacific Time. Re-read the status citation that morning. Send it once.

## What a sent item may be called

Use these sentences only after the named event.

| Event | Sentence |
| --- | --- |
| FCC comment is in ECFS and no order has addressed it | “I filed a comment in CG Docket No. 02-278 asking the FCC to define honoring a revocation as a permission record, cancellation of communications the caller still controls, and a check of that record at send time. The Commission has not adopted that text.” |
| Office of Administrative Law email or letter is sent | “I submitted a comment on the Office of Administrative Law’s proposed file-and-print rules so that a regulation exempt from that Office’s review can still be filed. The Office has not adopted the change.” |
| Pregnancy comment is sent | “I asked the Civil Rights Council to correct its statement of reasons so the qualified-employee definition stays at section 11035(p). The Council has not issued a final statement of reasons.” |
| Early Start comment is sent | “I filed a comment asking the Department of Developmental Services not to require certified mail 30 days before notice to a parent of a proposed or refused change in early intervention services. The Department has not adopted the text I requested.” |
| DROP comment is sent | “I asked the California Privacy Protection Agency to cite the multiple-consumer opt-out in the data-broker status a consumer reads. The Agency has not adopted that citation.” |
| A final statement of reasons rejects the comment | Name the document, the date, and the reason the agency gave. Do not describe the rejection as a partial adoption. |
| Adopted code matches the request | Describe the filed section. A hearing, a confirmation number, and a final statement of reasons are earlier events. |
