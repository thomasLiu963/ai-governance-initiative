# Adoption tracker

Research date: 2026-11-20. Status `draft` means the text exists in this folder and has not been sent. Status `watch` means a date or a body is real and no letter should be written until a posted agenda names the policy. Status `closed` means the reading found on November 20 is not an open adoption. Status `not-a-filing` means the channel cannot produce the institutional act this project is counting.

Nothing in this tracker has been submitted. Acceptance is empty until the evidence column has a public record of the body's own decision.

The internal checkpoint is Monday, November 30, 2026. On that date the decision is which of queue items 1, 3, and 4 in `OUTREACH_QUEUE.md` may be sent. Queue item 1 has to move in the first days of December if December 11 is the Tulare target.

## What counts

| Body | The act that counts | What does not count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USAC | Posted minutes showing a majority of the entire voting membership, and a packet listing three Council sponsors and the text | A sponsor's yes, a sent email, a floor speech, a news article |
| Tulare County Board | Minutes reading "Consideration and Approval, Second Reading" for Board Policy 0441, and the adopted policy text | A first reading, a mailed letter, a promise to review it |
| Pasadena Unified | Minutes of the adoption vote, and the policy posted in the district manual with the sentences the board voted | A first reading, a committee discussion, an archived comment |
| Capistrano Unified | Minutes of the vote that adopts the revised policy, and the posted text | Staff's October 15 statement that a revision would return |
| Palos Verdes Peninsula Unified | Minutes of the second-reading vote, and the posted policy | A first reading and a secondary summary that a second reading will occur |
| LAUSD | A later full-board action that adopts the recommended text | A committee comment, a survey, a presentation |
| UCSA | Posted board minutes naming the resolution and the vote | An Agenda Item Request, or a USAC request that UCSA consider one |
| UCLA Academic Senate or Chancellor | The amended regulation or the amended Conduct Code | A USAC resolution asking them to consider text |
| North Westwood, LANCC, WRAC | Not counted toward the February 15 adoption. A passed motion would be that body's position only. | A stakeholder request |
