Pasadena's November 19, 2026 meeting occurred. Pasadena Now's November 20 account reports the board's vote on the transformation plan. It does not report a vote on Board Policy 0441. December 24 is a real listed meeting and an unconfirmed second reading.

## What each yes vote is

**USAC.** Bylaws Article VI, Section A.5.a and A.5.b: a resolution expresses the opinion of the current Council. Section A.5.c: at least three members of Council are listed as sponsors. Section A.5.d: passage is a majority of the entire voting membership. Constitution Article II, Section D.1: the President votes only in a tie or where the vote affects a constitutionally required two-thirds or three-fourths. The other elected officers vote. Appointed and ex-officio members do not vote. The bylaws do not say whether the President is inside the phrase "entire voting membership" on a vote the President does not cast. This file does not convert that phrase into a number of yes votes. During the academic year, two-thirds of the officers constitute a quorum. Two-thirds of the fifteen listed offices is ten if every seat is filled. The November 17 agenda names an officer on each of the fifteen report lines. That is not a certificate that every seat is filled.

The Conduct Code changes if the Chancellor adopts an amendment after the consultation and the Office of the President review the Code already requires. Divisional Regulation A-306 changes if the Los Angeles Division of the Academic Senate amends it or issues a legislative ruling. The resolution asks those bodies to consider text.

**Tulare County Board of Education.** A "Consideration and Approval, Second Reading" vote adopts a policy of the county office. The office operates its own programs. The policy does not amend Visalia Unified or any other district in the county. A letter is correspondence. A board member moves the sentences.

**Pasadena Unified.** The Governing Board adopts district policy. The board-meetings page says a person may ask the Office of the Board of Education to place a matter on an agenda, the written request is due at least 12 days before the meeting, and the Superintendent or designee decides whether the matter is district business and when it is agendized. A written comment to publiccomment@pusd.us is archived and is not read aloud. A trustee moves an amendment. This research did not open a Pasadena bylaw that states a two-reading rule in those words. The November 19 item was described, before the meeting, as a first reading.
