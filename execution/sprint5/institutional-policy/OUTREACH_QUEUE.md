Subject: Comment for the second reading of Board Policy 0441, Artificial Intelligence

Members of the Board,

On November 4, 2026, the Board held a first reading of Board Policy 0441, Artificial Intelligence. Please add the following paragraphs to the policy before the second-reading vote. They fit after the policy's principles and before the direction to the County Superintendent.

"A grade, a disciplinary decision, or a change in a student's placement or program is made by a person. An artificial-intelligence output may inform that person. That person is able to reach a different result. The output does not itself assign the grade, impose the discipline, or change the placement.

A score, percentage, highlight, or other output of a tool that classifies student work as generated by artificial intelligence is not the sole basis for a grade penalty, a disciplinary decision, or a change in placement or program. Before any of those consequences, the student receives the applicable rule, the name of the tool, the date it was used, the output, and a meaningful chance to respond, including with drafts or other process evidence. The written decision states the evidence other than the output and states how the student's response was weighed.

Student work is not submitted to a generative-AI detection service the County Office has not approved for that purpose.

When the County Office requires a student to use an artificial-intelligence tool for an assignment and the student cannot use that tool, the County Office provides a way to complete the same assignment without it.

A student or parent may use the County Office's existing complaint, grievance, or appeal procedure to challenge a grade penalty, disciplinary decision, or placement change that relied on an artificial-intelligence output. The reviewer states in writing the evidence other than that output."

The paragraphs do not ban a tool. They state who decides, what a detection output cannot do by itself, what the student receives, how the student can challenge the result, and which existing procedure applies.

Please include this letter with the December 11, 2026 materials if Board Policy 0441 is on that agenda, and with the second-reading materials if the policy returns at a later meeting.

Subject: Sponsor inquiry: human review when an AI output is used in a student decision

Commissioner Ledesma-Grattarola,

I am an undergraduate, and I am writing because the Academic Affairs office represents students on educational policy to the administration and faculty. I would like to ask whether you will be the primary sponsor of a USAC resolution, with two other Council members.

The resolution is the Council's opinion. It would not amend the Student Conduct Code or Regulation A-306, and it would not decide any student's case. It asks the Academic Senate and the Chancellor to consider four protections when a detection score or other artificial-intelligence output is used in a conduct finding, a grade penalty, or a change in placement: a person makes the decision and can reach a different result; the output is not the sole basis; the student receives the rule, the tool, the date, the output, and a meaningful chance to respond; and the student uses the existing appeal or grade grievance, with the reviewer stating the other evidence.

The Code already prohibits unauthorized or unattributed generative-AI use and already requires proof by a preponderance. Regulation A-306 already requires a report and a Deferred Report while suspected cheating is pending. Neither text states what a detection output is worth.

Three Council sponsors and a majority of the entire voting membership are required. I have not asked anyone else. If you are willing, the filing I am aiming at is an email from you to the Internal Vice President by noon on Thursday, January 7, 2027, with the full text in the packet, for a January 12 agenda. The current bylaws set that noon deadline. The older resolution guide's Friday 3:00 p.m. instruction would be late for that Tuesday.

I can send the one-page resolution if you want to read it. If this is not something you can sponsor, I will not follow up with you.
