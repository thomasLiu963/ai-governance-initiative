## How to approve the insurance comment

`approve CDI comment` sends the letter in `CDI_SUBMISSION.md`, and only the letter below the line marked “Letter.”

Before that phrase is used:

- Put the commenter’s name on the letter.
- Put an email on the letter only if that address may appear in a public record. The invitation says the comment and the commenter’s address, telephone, and email can be released under the California Public Records Act. Leave the telephone blank.
- Send one email to CDIRegulations@insurance.ca.gov. Subject: `REG-2026-00007 — Written comment, Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence in Insurance`.
- Do not also mail a copy.
- Do not attach this queue, the cover, or the other files in this directory.
- The deadline in the invitation is December 12, 2026. The invitation does not state an hour.

Any other reply is not approval. Approval of the insurance comment does not approve a petition, the opt-out draft, or a message to GovOps, the Labor Commissioner, the Department of Education, or the Office of Emergency Services.

## What each result can be called

| Event | Sentence that matches the event |
| --- | --- |
| The insurance email is sent and no answer has arrived | “I submitted a written comment in the Department of Insurance’s December 2026 workshop on the responsible use of artificial intelligence in insurance. The invitation says the workshop is not a notice of proposed action and that the Department is not required to respond.” |
| The Department writes that it received the email | “The Department of Insurance acknowledged receipt of my workshop comment. It has not proposed a regulation.” |
| A later notice of proposed action is published, and the comment is resubmitted on time | “I commented in the Department of Insurance’s noticed rulemaking. The Department must answer that comment in the final statement of reasons. It has not yet done so.” Use this sentence only for the resubmission. |
| The final statement of reasons rejects the resubmitted comment | “The Department’s final statement of reasons explained why it did not adopt the examination records I requested.” |
| A filed title 10 regulation contains the three records | “The Department of Insurance adopted a regulation requiring an insurer that uses an artificial-intelligence output for an adverse underwriting decision or a claim denial to keep a human-review record, a version-locked evaluation record, and, where a vendor supplies the system, those records in the insurer’s own file.” |
| The adopted opt-out rule matches the combined-header sentence | “The California Privacy Protection Agency adopted a rule that a business receiving the `Sec-GPC` header must treat a combined field value containing `1` as the consumer’s opt-out.” |
| GovOps posts criteria that include the reproducibility record | “The Government Operations Agency’s posted designation criteria require an independent verification organization to identify the record a second person would use to repeat each methodology.” The order’s date for that posting is May 1, 2027. |

A workshop, a file stamp, and a final statement of reasons are different events. The table is the ceiling for each one.
