## Why it fits the open proceeding

The Federal Register synopsis, reconfirmed November 20, still says comments are due on or before **November 25, 2026**, and replies on or before December 28, 2026. Public Notice DA 26-1159, released October 27, 2026, states the same dates. No extension notice was found. The Further Notice asks whether to shorten the ten-business-day honor period, including for exclusive-means callers. The adopted paragraphs require a revocation to be “honored” and do not say what honoring is. The comment keeps the ten-day maximum and defines the three operations the questions do not supply: a permission record at receipt, cancellation of communications still in the caller’s control, and a read of that record at send time.

The comment does not ask for seven business days, a one-way ban, or the affiliate paragraph in footnote 84.
