The November 19, 2026 agenda is now posted, clip 1006 on the district’s Granicus archive. The item is under “Board Policies, Administrative Regulations, and Board Bylaws — First Read,” titled “New – Board Policy 0441 – Artificial Intelligence.” The exhibit is the October 2026 board-policy review submitted by Dr. Cham Hill, at https://pusd.granicus.com/MetaViewer.php?view_id=12&clip_id=1006&meta_id=170684. No email for Dr. Hill is printed on that coversheet.

The exhibit is still a first-reading draft. It is not an adopted policy. The Granicus publisher page, re-read the afternoon of November 20, lists two later meetings and no longer lists December 24 as the next meeting:

- Board of Education Annual Organizational Meeting, Monday, December 7, 2026, 5:00 p.m.
- Board of Education Meeting, Thursday, December 10, 2026, 5:00 p.m.

Neither agenda was posted. Regular-meeting agendas are posted on Friday evenings. For December 10, that evening is **Friday, December 4, 2026**. Do not aim the amendment at the December 7 organizational meeting unless that agenda, once posted, contains Board Policy 0441.

**Already in the draft, so do not ask for it again.**

- Professional duties, including assessment and grading, remain the responsibility of qualified staff. AI output on those decisions “shall serve solely as advisory input, subject to independent review.”
- Where AI-assisted enrichment or intervention is offered, the district shall provide non-AI instructional alternatives.
- Guardians may request human review of AI outputs before those outputs are used for school decisions.

**Not in the draft.** No sentence says a detection output is not the sole basis for a grade penalty, a disciplinary decision, or a change in placement. No sentence requires notice of the rule, the tool, the date, and the output, or a chance to respond, before those consequences. No sentence points the student or parent to the existing complaint procedure and requires the reviewer to state the other evidence. No sentence bars an upload to a detector the district has not approved. Discipline and placement are outside the grading sentence the draft already has.

**Who can act.** Kimberly Kenne chairs the Board Policy Committee and can move the sentences. Tina Fredericks and Scott Harden sit on that committee. Dr. Cham Hill is the named submitter of the draft and is the staff person who can insert the sentences before it returns. The published comment address, from the board-meetings page, is publiccomment@pusd.us. The board office phone is 626.396.3600. No separate email for Kenne or Hill was verified.

### Email — do not send

To: publiccomment@pusd.us
Subject: Public Comment — add four sentences to the second reading of Board Policy 0441

Members of the Board,

Board Policy 0441 had its first reading on November 19. I am asking Board Member Kimberly Kenne to move the four sentences below at the second reading, and I am asking Dr. Cham Hill to include them in the draft that returns. A comment does not itself amend the policy.

Please do not change the sentences already in the draft that keep grading with qualified staff, treat AI output on those duties as advisory, provide a non-AI alternative for enrichment or intervention, and let a guardian request human review. Please add:

“A score, percentage, highlight, or other output of a tool that classifies student work as generated by artificial intelligence is not the sole basis for a grade penalty, a disciplinary decision, or a change in a student’s placement or program. Before any of those consequences, the student receives the applicable rule, the name of the tool, the date it was used, the output, and a meaningful chance to respond, including with drafts or other process evidence. The written decision states the evidence other than the output and states how the student’s response was weighed.

Student work is not submitted to a generative-AI detection service the district has not approved for that purpose.

A student or parent may use the district’s existing complaint procedure to challenge a grade penalty, disciplinary decision, or placement change that relied on an artificial-intelligence output. The reviewer states in writing the evidence other than that output.”

Please include this comment in the December 10, 2026 materials if Board Policy 0441 is on that agenda.

**Acceptance.** The minutes of the meeting that adopts Board Policy 0441, and the posted policy, containing these sentences. A first reading and this email are not adoption.

### Email to the presenters — do not send

To: gilberth@tcoe.org, valarie.seita@tcoe.org
Subject: Four sentences for the second reading of Board Policy 0441, if they are not already in the draft

Mr. Hernandez and Ms. Seita,

You presented the first reading of Board Policy 0441 on November 4. I could not open the policy text in the packet. “A grade, a disciplinary decision, or a change in a student’s placement or program is made by a person. An artificial-intelligence output may inform that person. A score, percentage, highlight, or other output of a tool that classifies student work as generated by artificial intelligence is not the sole basis for a grade penalty, a disciplinary decision, or a change in placement or program. Before any of those consequences, the student receives the applicable rule, the name of the tool, the date it was used, the output, and a meaningful chance to respond. The written decision states the evidence other than the output.

Student work is not submitted to a generative-AI detection service the County Office has not approved for that purpose.

A student or parent may use the County Office’s existing complaint procedure to challenge a grade penalty, disciplinary decision, or placement change that relied on an artificial-intelligence output. The reviewer states in writing the evidence other than that output.”

I am not asking you to place a vote on the December 11 agenda if Board Policy 0441 is not returning that day.
